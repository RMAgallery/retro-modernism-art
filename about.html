About us

Retro Modernism Art is a small art studio making minimalist prints about the world we're living in — the scroll, the grind, the noise.

Our style is low-poly 2D with one simple rule: complex concepts, communicated through uncomplex images. No text, no frames, no clutter — just a cream background, a restrained palette, and one small spark of hope hidden in every piece.

The Choose Collection

Six original pieces — Choose Connection, Choose Presence, Choose Creativity, Choose Humility, Choose Nature, and Choose Real — each available as an 18×12, 24×16, or 30×20 matte print.

Made to order

Every print is made to order on quality matte poster stock and shipped straight to your door. Prints arrive unframed and fit standard off-the-shelf frames. Please allow up to 15 days for production and delivery.

For hosts & property managers

Furnishing a short-term rental? The Full Stay Collection bundles all six pieces in mixed sizes — one order, one cohesive look, walls done. See the collection.
